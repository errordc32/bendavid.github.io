## Overview

Endoscopic submucosal dissection (ESD) removes lesions through an endoscope, so the cutting tool has to be very small. I designed and fabricated a **sub-millimeter electrosurgical knife** for these procedures.

## What I did

- **Designed** the knife geometry to fit sub-millimeter size constraints.
- **Fabricated** the tool in-house.
- Demonstrated its use in ESD procedures.
