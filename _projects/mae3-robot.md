## Overview

MAE 3 is UCSD's introductory engineering design course. Teams design, build, and compete with a small mobile robot. Our robot collects game pieces into a large hopper and uses a pivoting arm to load and deliver them.

## What I did

- **Modeled the full robot in CAD**: a gusseted chassis, a sheet-style hopper, and a spur-gear drivetrain.
- **Fabricated** the structure from laser-cut panels joined with fasteners and standoffs, so parts could be swapped quickly between test runs.
- Used **omni wheels** in the front for tight turning and geared drive wheels in the back for traction.
- Designed a **pivoting arm** with linkage plates to load and deliver game pieces.

## Outcome

The robot went from CAD to a working competition build in one quarter. It was my first full design–fabricate–test cycle.
