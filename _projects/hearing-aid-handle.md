## Overview

Hearing aids are tiny. For someone with arthritis, tremor, or limited hand strength, just picking one up and putting it in can be the hardest part of using it. Through TOM at UCSD, I designed a **wearable handling aid** so they can do it without help.

## What I did

- **Designed a clip** that sits between the thumb and index finger and stays in place without needing a grip.
- Added a **post that holds the hearing aid**, so the user can guide it into place with whole-hand movements instead of a fingertip pinch.
- Shaped the clip around a hand model so it fits comfortably and can be **3D printed** cheaply in different sizes.
