## Overview

Alongside the [3D-printed wheelchair]({{ '/projects/wheelchair-3d-printed/' | relative_url }}), TOM at UCSD built a **plywood version** of the pediatric chair. Plywood takes less specialized equipment to build, so more makers can produce it.

## What I did

- Built the chair from slotted plywood panels, with a padded seat, a five-point harness, and a footrest that adjusts as the child grows.
- Paired the plywood frame with large self-propel rear wheels and front casters.
