## Overview

Running blades are expensive, which keeps many people out of para-athletic sports. Through TOM, I worked with the Jacobs School of Engineering on a **low-cost, open-source running blade**.

## What I did

- **Designed** the blade for low-cost fabrication.
- Released it as **open source** so others can build and adapt it.

## Outcome

More independence for amputee athletes, and a much lower cost of entry.
