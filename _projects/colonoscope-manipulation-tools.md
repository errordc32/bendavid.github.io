## Overview

Handling a colonoscope is physically demanding and can introduce risk. In the Delson Lab I designed tools to make it easier and built instrumentation to measure the effect.

## What I did

- **Designed** external colonoscope manipulation tools that reduce stress and risk during operation.
- **Researched tribology techniques** to analyze and reduce friction in the system.
- **Designed and assembled a portable testing rig** that collects **torque and EMG data** on the advanced colonoscopy simulator at the Center for Surgery of the Future.
